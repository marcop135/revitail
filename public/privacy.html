Privacy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This notice covers the website at vitereact.marcopontili.com. The starter template you download from GitHub runs on your own machine and is outside this notice.

Controller

Marco Pontili. Contact: marcopontili.com/contact.

Analytics

This site uses a self-hosted installation of Matomo, run by the controller. It is configured as follows:

  • No cookies are set and nothing is stored on your device.
  • IP addresses are anonymised before they are stored.
  • If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, no request is recorded.
  • Data processed: anonymised IP address, pages visited, referrer, links clicked, browser and device type, time of visit.

Purpose: aggregate measurement of how the site is used, to improve it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in privacy-friendly, aggregate traffic measurement. No profiles are built and the data is not shared with third parties.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netsons (Italy), which acts as processor and records standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) to deliver and secure the site. Requests pass through Cloudflare as a content delivery and security proxy. No Cloudflare analytics or tracking is enabled. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in operating a secure website.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no local storage for tracking. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Retention

Analytics data is kept only as long as it is useful for aggregate reporting. Server logs follow the host's standard rotation.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise any of these rights, use the contact page above. You may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live or work. The same rights and the same contact route apply wherever you are in the world.
